About

Synthetic Data for Learning Analytics

Learning analytics research is often constrained by limited access to authentic educational data because of ethical, legal, and privacy concerns. Synthetic data offers a way to generate artificial datasets that preserve useful statistical properties while protecting individual privacy.

Recent advances in generative AI, including large language models, diffusion models, and generative adversarial networks, have improved the quality and scale of synthetic data generation. This workshop focuses on practical methods, evaluation strategies, and the research challenges that remain.

Tabular Data

Generate synthetic educational datasets with CTGAN, SDV, and related methods.

Text Generation

Create synthetic assignments, feedback, and dialogues using large language models.

Visual Content

Explore multimodal and image generation methods for educational settings.
